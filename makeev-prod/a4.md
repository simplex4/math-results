No. The positive results are logically independent of the counterexample. The negative argument requires only:

1. the cyclic realization of the (A_4) root system;
2. the exact-edge-label criterion;
3. the cubic edge identity and its symmetry-annihilation consequence;
4. compactness of (SO(4));
5. the algebraic transversality proposition.

This matches the original counterexample draft, which explicitly says that it uses the exact-label criterion and cubic variational identity from the first part, rather than the subsequent positive theorems.  The general theorem for cubics and linear functions is a separate result.
